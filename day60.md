Day 60 – Mini DevOps Health Checker

What I Learned

- Combined functions, command-line arguments, conditions, and reusable script structure into one project.
- Used "elif" to check multiple service names.
- Built a simple health checker that behaves like a small DevOps utility.

Challenges Encountered

- I carefully tested the script with different arguments to ensure it handled valid services, unknown services, and missing arguments correctly.

Resolution

- By testing multiple scenarios, I confirmed that the script behaved as expected and produced the correct output in every case.

DevOps Lesson Learned

Reliable automation comes from writing scripts that handle different situations gracefully. Testing with multiple inputs is just as important as writing the script itself.
